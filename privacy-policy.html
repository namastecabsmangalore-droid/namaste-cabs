Privacy Policy

How we handle your booking information.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit a booking enquiry, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, pickup and destination details, travel date and other information you choose to provide.

2. How We Use Information

We use enquiry information to respond to customers, discuss travel arrangements, coordinate requested services and provide customer support.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as FormSubmit for email enquiry delivery and WhatsApp for customer communication. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to information submitted through their services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell customer contact information. Information may be shared when necessary to respond to a booking request, provide an agreed service or comply with applicable law.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to handle enquiry information responsibly. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about information you have provided or request correction of inaccurate details, subject to applicable requirements.
