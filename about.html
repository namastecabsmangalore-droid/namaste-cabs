About Namaste Cabs

OUR STORY

Travel With Confidence.

Namaste Cabs is a taxi-service brand based in Mangaluru, Karnataka. We aim to make travel enquiries and journey arrangements convenient for individuals, families and businesses.

From local travel and airport transfers to outstation journeys and tour enquiries, we help customers find a suitable travel arrangement.

Our approach

Clear communication, convenient booking and attention to customer travel requirements are central to the way we approach every enquiry.
