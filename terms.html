Important information about travel enquiries.

1. Booking Enquiries

Submitting a form, email or WhatsApp message is a booking enquiry only. It does not confirm a reservation. A booking is confirmed only after Namaste Cabs communicates acceptance and confirms the relevant details.

2. Fare and Payment

Fares, applicable charges, payment terms and any advance amount will be communicated and agreed before a booking is confirmed. No fare is guaranteed by this website unless specifically quoted and confirmed.

3. Vehicle Availability

Vehicle category, model, seating and availability are subject to confirmation. A requested vehicle is not guaranteed until the booking is confirmed.

4. Changes and Cancellations

Customers should contact Namaste Cabs as early as possible to request a change or cancellation. Any applicable cancellation charges or refund terms will be communicated at the time of booking confirmation.

5. Customer Responsibilities

Customers should provide accurate pickup, destination, contact and travel details. They should also communicate luggage, passenger and special travel requirements before confirmation.

6. Website Information

Service descriptions are general information and may be updated. Actual arrangements depend on the confirmed booking details.
